Superadmission is a proposed Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) designed to create a seamless, frictionless access to higher education admissions for every student in India.

Our ultimate goal is to ensure that no student ever misses a deserved admission due to <Tooltip tip="The frustration of system crashes, frozen payment gateways, and losing progress on slow websites during critical deadline hours.">technical barriers</Tooltip>, a lack of information, or a shortage of resources. Built on the principles of <Tooltip tip="Stripping away repetitive forms and multiple logins so that a student can complete in minutes what used to take weeks.">radical simplification</Tooltip> and <Tooltip tip="Safely connecting separate government and university systems so they can instantly talk to each other and share verified data with your permission.">secure interoperability</Tooltip>, Superadmission transforms a stressful maze of separate portals into a single, transparent network ensuring that merit, not administrative hurdles, dictates a student's future.

## The Three Layers

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Superadmission" icon="layer-group">
    The coordination layer that sits beneath JoSAA, MCC, and every state counselling body. It doesn't run counselling and it doesn't decide who gets which seat. It removes the repeated work a student currently does by hand between those systems, one profile, one document vault, one dashboard, instead of a separate version of each per counselling body.
  </Card>

  <Card title="PraveshAI" icon="brain">
    The engine underneath Superadmission that does the actual thinking: verifying identity and documents, estimating a student's real chances at a given college and answer students' doubts with verified information, matching students to seats, and keeping every deadline in sync.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Manifest" icon="database">
    A verified registry of every degree-granting institution in India, more than 70,000 of them. It answers the question a student has before anything else: what is this college, and what does it actually offer.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
