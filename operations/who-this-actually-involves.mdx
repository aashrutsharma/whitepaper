The earlier pages in this section followed one student's path through counselling in a lot of detail. That's useful for understanding how the system actually works step by step, but it can leave a misleading impression that this is built around one kind of student. It isn't. This page is a quick look at the actual range of people this has to work for.

  <Card title="The student in a low-connectivity area" icon="internet-explorer">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Across roughly 2,000 real student admission journeys studied while building this, a consistent pattern holds: a meaningful share of students who start the process never finish it, and it's rarely because they weren't eligible. It's a missed deadline, a confusing document rejection, or simply never finding out a seat had opened up in time.

This is why one profile, one document vault, and one dashboard aren't built around a single ideal student. They're built to hold up for whichever of these three situations a given student is actually in.
